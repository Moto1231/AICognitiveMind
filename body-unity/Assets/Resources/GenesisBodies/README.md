# Genesis Bodies

Place predefined Unity body assets here.

Supported library entries are Unity GameObject assets such as prefabs or
imported model GameObjects. Each asset is exposed in the desktop **Bodies**
selector by its Unity asset name.

Genesis itself remains the generated editable VRM body and does not belong in
this folder.

A predefined Genesis Body may be VRM or non-VRM. Speech and sensing do not
depend on avatar format. Visual lip sync is enabled when the asset exposes a
supported VRM mouth expression, common mouth/jaw blendshape, or compatible
mouth transform.
